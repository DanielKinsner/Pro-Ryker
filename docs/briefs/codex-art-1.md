# Art brief 1 for PRO RYKER (browser skate game, Tony Hawk's Pro Skater parody)

Use your image generation tool. Save every image as PNG at EXACTLY the paths below (create folders if needed).
Do not write any code. Do not modify any other files. When done, list the files you saved with pixel sizes.

Setting: a pink-tinted concrete skatepark on a bright autumn afternoon (red maples, blue sky with big clouds).
Tone: late-90s/early-2000s skate culture, punk zine, sticker-bombed, funny. NO real brand logos. NO real people.

1. public/assets/art/tags/tag-01.png … tag-08.png — eight separate graffiti pieces, each on a TRANSPARENT
   background, spray-paint look with drips/overspray, as if painted on concrete. Vary colors (black, white,
   teal, mustard yellow, purple, orange). Mostly abstract wildstyle letterforms; two of them may read
   "HOLD ON" and "NOPE". Square-ish, 1024x1024.
2. public/assets/art/stickers/sticker-01.png … sticker-06.png — six skate-sticker badges on TRANSPARENT
   backgrounds, thick white die-cut border, bold flat colors, 1024x1024:
   (01) a cartoon black three-wheeled motorcycle (two wheels in front, one in back) doing a flip;
   (02) a flaming motorcycle helmet with wings; (03) a traffic cone with a halo; (04) two leather-gloved hands
   white-knuckle gripping handlebars; (05) a grinning skull wearing an open-face helmet; (06) a smiley face
   with a bandage on its forehead.
3. public/assets/art/concrete-pink.png — SEAMLESS TILEABLE top-down photo texture of weathered pink/salmon
   tinted broom-finished skatepark concrete, subtle aggregate, faint scuffs, even lighting, no shadows, 1024x1024.
4. public/assets/art/concrete-pink-smooth.png — SEAMLESS TILEABLE top-down texture of smooth trowelled
   pink/salmon skatepark bowl concrete, very subtle mottling and wax sheen variation, even lighting, 1024x1024.
5. public/assets/art/title-bg.png — 1920x1080 (or 16:9) gritty punk-zine collage background: torn paper,
   halftone dots, masking tape, photocopy grain, pink concrete and red autumn leaf scraps. NO text, NO vehicles,
   NO people; leave the center calm so a logo can sit on top.
6. public/assets/art/loading.png — 16:9 comic-book illustration: a middle-aged biker in a black leather jacket
   and helmet has been thrown off the back of a black three-wheeled motorcycle (two wheels at the FRONT, one
   wheel at the back, sporty roadster) and is being dragged face-down behind it across a pink concrete skatepark
   bowl, both hands still gripping the handlebars, legs flailing, his cap flying off, onlooking skaters filming
   with phones. Red autumn trees, highway overpass in the background. Bold ink lines, halftone shading,
   dynamic motion lines, funny. NO text, NO logos.
