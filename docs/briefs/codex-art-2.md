# Art brief 2 for PRO RYKER — cardboard-cutout spectators

Use your image generation tool. Save every image as PNG at EXACTLY the paths below (create folders if needed).
Do not write any code. Do not modify any other files. When done, list the files you saved with pixel sizes.

These are life-size **cardboard cutout standees** of skatepark spectators (the joke: the park's crowd is literally
cardboard). Style: match the existing comic illustration `public/assets/art/loading.jpg` — bold ink outlines,
flat colours with halftone shading, late-90s skate zine energy, funny and expressive. Each image: ONE full-body
character, standing, feet at the bottom edge, centred, on a fully TRANSPARENT background, with a thin white
die-cut border around the silhouette (like a cut-out). Portrait 768x1536 (1:2). NO text, NO logos, NO real people.

For each character make an IDLE pose and a REACT pose that match (same person, same outfit, same scale and
framing, feet in the same place) so they can be swapped in-game.

1. public/assets/art/crowd/filmer-idle.png — a young woman in a red flannel over a black tee and baggy jeans,
   holding a phone up horizontally in both hands, filming, focused.
   public/assets/art/crowd/filmer-react.png — same woman, phone lowered to her chest, mouth open in disbelief.
2. public/assets/art/crowd/kid-idle.png — a lanky teenage skater in a black hoodie with bear ears on the hood,
   baggy cargo shorts, holding a skateboard vertically at his side, deadpan.
   public/assets/art/crowd/kid-react.png — same kid, skateboard raised overhead in one hand, other arm up, cheering.
3. public/assets/art/crowd/employee-idle.png — a tired middle-aged municipal park employee in a hi-vis vest over a
   green polo, khaki pants, clipboard under one arm, arms crossed, unimpressed.
   public/assets/art/crowd/employee-react.png — same employee, facepalming with one hand, clipboard dangling.
4. public/assets/art/crowd/skater2-idle.png — a skater girl with a mustard beanie, oversized white tee, checkered
   pants, sitting-height? NO — standing, holding a board under one arm, chewing gum.
   public/assets/art/crowd/skater2-react.png — same skater, both hands on her head in shock, board dropped at her feet.
